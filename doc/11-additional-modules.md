# 10. Suggested Additional Modules

- **Main Dashboard** (cross-company): cash position across all bank accounts, income vs expense trend, top overdue invoices, upcoming reminders, budget alerts, active loans summary.
- **Payroll-lite** (if not handled elsewhere): salary as a recurring expense category, linked to departments — optional given you may already have an HR system.
- **Approval inbox**: single screen listing everything awaiting someone's confirmation (draft invoices, over-budget expenses, loan closures).
- **User activity/audit log viewer** for admins.

*(Financial/statutory reports have their own dedicated file — see `10-reports-module.md`.)*
